# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Cypra welcoming and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Prefer technical evidence over personal criticism.
- Assume good intent, then clarify quickly when intent is unclear.
- Keep security reports private until maintainers coordinate disclosure.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith security disclosure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict access for behavior that violates this code. Security-sensitive conduct issues can be reported through the security contact in `SECURITY.md`.
